05 / ABOUT

  • Model: PiVo-TPU-7M Hebrew v3: 6,511,317 acoustic + 816,597 FARGAN vocoder parameters (7,327,914 total). ONNX files on device: 29.5 MB (float32).
  • Training: about 11.5 h of Hebrew speech (SASPEECH), 20-dim FARGAN features at 16 kHz, trained on a Kaggle TPU v5e-8.
  • Text: typed text is read by ReNikud (CC BY 4.0), a 20.6 MB Hebrew pronunciation model that runs in your browser and predicts vowels and stress from the whole sentence. Numbers, dates, times, prices, common abbreviations and English words are read out. Speech pauses at punctuation and at clause boundaries, and yes/no questions rise at the end. Vowel points (niqqud) you type are respected.
  • Limits: an experimental research model: some speech sounds muffled or buzzy, and pitch is fairly flat. About 1 word in 8 may still be mispronounced, mostly names, slang and rare loanwords. Adding niqqud to a word fixes its pronunciation.

Based on PiVo-1.4M by Empero Research (Apache-2.0; FARGAN vocoder under the Xiph/Opus BSD license, see NOTICE, THIRD_PARTY.md). Page design inspired by ldenoue/PiVo-1.4M-web. Runs on ONNX Runtime Web. Reference recordings from SASPEECH. Pronunciation: ReNikud by Melichov, Kolani and Alper (CC BY 4.0).
